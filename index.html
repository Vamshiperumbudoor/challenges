ABOUT TISC

Your Innovation.
Telangana’s Next Big Solution.

The Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) is a flagship initiative of the Government of Telangana that brings together startups, innovators, researchers and government departments to address some of Telangana’s most pressing real-world challenges.

More than an innovation competition, TISC offers an opportunity to solve meaningful problems, engage directly with government stakeholders, validate solutions in real-world environments and explore pathways to government adoption and commercial deployment.

FAQs

Questions,
answered.

Everything you need to know before you apply. Exact requirements for each challenge are subject to departmental confirmation.

Who can apply to TISC?

Startups at any stage, individual innovators and entrepreneurs, and student and university research teams.

What is the last date to apply?

Applications open on 29 October 2026 and close on 20 November 2026. Demo Day is on 1 December 2026 at the T-Hub campus, Hyderabad, and selected startups take part in the Startup Showcase from 7 to 9 December 2026.

How will solutions be evaluated?

Shortlisting is based on feasibility, innovation, affordability, scalability and public impact.

Will I get to present at the Global Summit?

Selected teams may present their solutions at the Invest Telangana Global Summit 2026 to government leaders, global investors and industry.

Can I apply for multiple use cases or target sectors?

No, you can only apply for 1 use case/sector at a time per registration. Choose the primary sector that best aligns with your core innovation.

I am not from Hyderabad or Telangana. Can I still apply?

Yes, applicants from all across India and global hubs are welcome to apply.

Will the selection process be conducted online or offline?

The 1st level screening and initial evaluation will be held online. Only shortlisted candidates will be required to attend the final pitch round and Demo Day offline at the T-Hub campus, Hyderabad.

Can both software (AI/IT) and hardware (non-IT) innovations apply?

Yes. TISC supports cross-sector innovation spanning pure software/AI solutions, hybrid IoT models, and non-IT hardware/materials science innovations.

What parameters will the screening panel use to evaluate applications?

Submissions will be assessed based on problem relevance, innovation uniqueness, Technology Readiness Level, team capability, and potential impact on public/industrial sectors.

Does selection guarantee a government contract, PoC, purchase order or funding?

No. Selection does not guarantee a government contract, Proof of Concept (PoC), Purchase Order (PO) or funding. Solutions may be considered by the relevant Government of Telangana departments for further evaluation. Any subsequent engagement would be subject to departmental requirements, applicable processes and approvals.
